# Failure Codes

Use these codes in ledgers and reports.

- `KIG_READINESS_LEAK`: a readiness, production, release, or generation flag is true.
- `KIG_SOURCE_ANCHOR_MISSING`: candidatepack eligibility is claimed without source refs.
- `KIG_HARD_CLAIM_LEAK`: hard claim text appears without evidence routing.
- `KIG_BRAND_FACT_LEAK`: concrete brand, product, store, city, person, campaign, or customer feedback leaks.
- `KIG_RICH_BODY_MISSING_BLOCK`: required rich_body_blocks field is missing.
- `KIG_RICH_BODY_OVERREACH`: body text exceeds candidate boundaries.
- `KIG_DUPLICATE_UNRESOLVED`: semantic fingerprint duplicate lacks supersession or reject routing.
- `KIG_CAPABILITY_ROUTE_MISSING`: capability refs or consumption hint are absent.
- `KIG_SERVING_TEXT_GENERATED`: approved passage text, rendered body, or passage text is generated.
- `KIG_GOLD_SYSTEM_FORK`: gold hook candidate claims to be an independent release system.
