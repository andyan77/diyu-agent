# Semantic Pilot V4.7 Judge Protocol

Judge review must decide whether the eight V4.7 review-pending drafts solved the targeted semantic cleanup issues. The review must not unlock batch generation, CandidatePack, KE, Serving, RAG, or DIFY.

Required review gates:

- Knowledge capsule body fields contain no governance or route-process language.
- Claim boundary cards keep direct observation, conditional experience, source-required performance claim, and prohibited guarantee separate.
- Control-plane cards use route, guard, ledger, outbox, and reentry consumers only.
- Source gaps block formal landing but do not block clearly labeled review-pending drafts.
- Relation hints remain design hints, not ontology edges.
