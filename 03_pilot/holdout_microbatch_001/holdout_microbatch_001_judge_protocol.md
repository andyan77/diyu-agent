# Holdout Microbatch 001 Judge Protocol

Judge review checks whether the V4.7 repair direction transfers to new W7 clusters. This is pilot validation only and must not unlock formal batch generation.

- Confirm all selected clusters are new relative to V4-V4.7 sampled clusters.
- Confirm every draft has the full asset bundle, not an index-only capsule.
- Confirm rich bodies contain domain body now and do not rely on future evidence to create their mechanism.
- Confirm no real brand, SKU, store, person, price, inventory, or direct publish script appears.
- Confirm relation hints remain design hints, not ontology edges.
